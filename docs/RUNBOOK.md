# Ops Runbook (Daily/Weekly)

## Daily
- Review escalations (damage/refund/legal/medical) first
- Check today's bookings + address/zip validation
- Scan new reviews and respond within SLA

## Weekly
- Review KPI dashboard: conversion rate, handoff rate, review rate
- Audit pricing drift: compare Fieldd catalog vs KB pricing_matrix.json
- Retrain intents with new customer phrasing

## Monthly
- Refresh trust proof registry
- SEO + local landing page updates (see marketing playbook)
